MCA — Middle East Combat Arena

Offline greybox skirmish RTS · single-file build
To play: double-click MCA.html. No install, no server, no internet — the whole game is inside that one file. Share it by email/chat as-is.

What it is

MCA is a single-player skirmish RTS set in a near-future Middle East conflict. You start with one MCV; deploy it, build power and oil income, raise a combined-arms force from your faction's real-world order of battle (161 units across 9 factions), and destroy the AI's Construction Yard and MCV. Fully offline — skirmish vs AI opponents (optional AI ally for 2v2).

How a match works

1. Select your MCV and press D (or double-click it) to deploy the Construction Yard.
2. Build from the bottom bar: Power Plant first, then an Oil Depot next to an oil field (gold rings show them while placing), then production structures.
3. Structures must sit within the build-radius circle of your base. If you click a slightly-off spot, placement snaps to the nearest legal cell; if nothing nearby works, a red label at the cursor says why. Hold Shift while placing to chain multiple defenses.
4. Watch the power dot in the top bar — green is fine, amber is close to the limit, red means low power (production slows).
5. Right-click the map with a production structure selected to set a rally point — fresh units auto-move there.

Tips

  • Income comes from Oil Depots — each one spawns its own harvester. Expand early, defend them; kill an enemy harvester and their income stalls.
  • Hover any build tile for cost, build time, HP, and what beats it / threatens it.
  • Engineers repair your buildings — and can capture enemy or neutral ones: order them to attack the structure and hold the 2-second channel.
  • Lose your Construction Yard? You get one rebuild — keep some credits in reserve.
  • The Command / Tech structure (SH-08) unlocks the expensive tier-2/3 units.
  • Faction abilities need their unique building (the button tells you which) — e.g. Egypt's Sa'ka Insertion needs the Sa'ka Tent.
  • Superweapons charge for 5 minutes once built — the button lives in the build bar's right column.
  • Each faction plays differently — read the identity line on the faction picker. The full unit/structure reference is in MCA_Reference.html.
