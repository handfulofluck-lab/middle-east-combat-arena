
ADDENDUM TO GDD v0.1
ASH-SHARQ: DESERT COMMAND
v0.2  —  FIVE REGIONAL POWERS
Turkey · Israel · India · Pakistan · IranRead this on top of GDD v0.1. It does not replace the original four Arab armies.
This addendum expands the launch fantasy from ‘Arab armies only’ to ‘the neighbourhood’. The original four (Egypt, Saudi, UAE, Algeria) stay the identity core. The five new factions are full playable armies with the same rules: published-inventory anchors, one unique mechanic, one hero, one superweapon, dialect/language voice pack including Uncut 18+ lines, Family/Military/Uncut slider.
0. How to ship nine factions without dying
Nine complete art-and-voice factions is a sequel-sized project. Do not pretend otherwise. Recommended ship plan:

0.1 What changes in the original vision
Subtitle on the box can become DESERT COMMAND / REGIONAL POWERS. Working title ASH-SHARQ still works: the map is the East as seen from Alexandria, not a UN atlas.
Al-Mizan (the fictional private network) now also stokes India–Pakistan and Israel–Iran crises in the same month, so campaigns can cross. No living politician is named. No nuclear weapons. No depiction of real terror organisations as playable sides.
Ladder: any faction vs any faction. Mirror matches allowed. Map pool expands (see §6).
Voice rule is now ‘language of the army’, not ‘Arabic only’. UI stays English + Arabic + the new languages if budget allows. Combat VO is always native.
Legal: inspired-by markings only. No official IDF, TSK, IAF, PAF, or IRGC emblems without a licence you will not get.

1. Turkey — Türk Silahlı Kuvvetleri
Doctrine: NATO-sized army that decided to build its own toys. The player should feel Baykar / Roketsan / Aselsan as a system: a TB2 is cheap and annoying, an Akıncı is a flying artillery piece, a Siper battery is a poor man’s S-400 that still ruins casual air play. Ground game is Leopard / M60T now, Altay later in the match.
Unique mechanic — SİHA NETWORK: every friendly UAV shares a ‘mesh’. Spotted enemies stay painted for 8 seconds after the drone leaves LOS. TB2s are expendable scouts; losing one is not a tragedy. Akıncı can lase for Fırtına / Kasırga. This is not Saudi off-map jets. This is organic, on-map drone weather.
Hero — YÜZBAŞI EMRE ‘KARA’ YILMAZ: commando (Bordo Bereliler flavour). Can deploy a portable anti-drone jammer (small radius, 12s) and a satchel. Voice: western-Anatolian, dry, swears when the net is closed.
Superweapon — TAYFUN RAIN: a conventional SRBM/QRSAM-supported strike on a medium radius. Late: SİPER + a Kızılelma UCAV package that hunts remaining air. Interceptable by Iron Dome / S-400 / Patriot / Bavar.
Colour: NATO green + red flag trim + light grey drones. UI accent crimson.

2. Israel — Israel Defense Forces
Doctrine: Small, expensive, over-trained, and wrapped in a defensive bubble that makes casual rocket spam look stupid. Ground game is Merkava + Namer — the heaviest infantry taxi in the nine. Air game is F-15I / F-16I / F-35I. The player who skips AA against Israel eats Trophy-deflected ATGMs and then an Adir.
Unique mechanic — IRON LAYER: incoming rockets, cruise and some ballistic shots in a radius around Iron Dome / David’s Sling / Arrow structures have a high intercept chance. The player spends a small credit trickle per intercept (the ‘Tamir bill’). Trophy APS on Merkava and Namer can eat ONE ATGM every 20 seconds. Israel does not get cheap units. Israel gets to live through the first volley.
Hero — RAV SEREN NOA ‘SHACHAF’ BEN-AMI: Sayeret-flavour operator. Female hero, distinct silhouette. Short cloak, Spike-SR, can call a single precise Delilah-flavour loiter. Voice: dry Hebrew, cuts to street when hit.
Superweapon — ADIR PACKAGE: F-35I + F-15I coordinated bunker-delete on a small radius (UAE-like precision, higher air threat). Late: Arrow shot that can kill an inbound superweapon. Iron Beam flavour as a support power vs drones/rockets, not a death laser on tanks — the real system is still maturing, so keep it honest: excellent vs cheap air, weak vs armour.
Colour: Sinai grey-green + black/white. UI accent blue-white. Do not use the exact Star of David as a decal; use a chevron + bar.
NOTE: Campaign tone for this faction is defensive / counter-strike against Al-Mizan false flags, not a documentary of any real war. No real unit numbers from current conflicts on loading screens. No playable militia factions.

3. India — Indian Armed Forces
Doctrine: The biggest mixed catalogue on the roster after Egypt, but heavier. T-90 Bhishma wall, Arjun as a proud expensive oddity, Su-30MKI numbers, Rafale quality, BrahMos as the sentence everyone understands. Naval maps can grow an aircraft carrier late, which no other faction gets as a unit (Egypt’s Mistral is a helicopter carrier fantasy, not a jet deck).
Unique mechanic — BRAHMOS LANE: from the Cruise Battery, the player plots a LOW-ALTITUDE LINE on the map. The missile follows it. It can be intercepted if SAM sees the corridor. This is a skill-shot super-cruise, not a click-delete. Teaches map reading.
Hero — COLONEL ARJUN ‘SHERPA’ THAPA: mountain / para flavour (fictional name, not a real CO). Bonus on elevation. Can mark a peak for artillery. Voice: crisp Indian English + Hindi snap when angry.
Superweapon — TRISHUL: BrahMos salvo + Su-30 package on a medium radius. Late: a carrier air wing strike on naval maps, or a second BrahMos lofted shot on land maps.
Colour: olive + saffron-white-green trim used sparingly. Do not paint roundels as the exact IAF roundel; use a stylised orange-white-green bar.

4. Pakistan — Pakistan Armed Forces
Doctrine: Lighter than India, meaner in the first ten minutes. JF-17 numbers, J-10C quality, F-16 veterans, Al-Khalid / VT-4 (Haider) armour, Babur cruise, Hangor AIP submarines, a growing Chinese/Turkish shopping list. They win by hitting first and denying the sky with HQ-series / indigenous AD, not by a T-90 parking lot.
Unique mechanic — BABUR WINDOW: a cruise corridor like India’s BrahMos lane, but cheaper, slower, and stackable. Player can put two Baburs in the air. Also: JF-17s rebuild 15% faster than other light fighters (PAC Kamra flavour).
Hero — MAJOR BILAL ‘SHER’ QURESHI: SSG flavour. Can call a short artillery smoke + a single loitering munition. Voice: Urdu, Punjabi colour when angry.
Superweapon — GHAZNAVI RAIN: conventional SRBM + Babur mix on a large radius. Late: Hangor Club-flavour land-attack from the sea if a sub is alive, or a J-10C + JF-17 Block III package.
Colour: olive drab + green crescent trim (stylised, not the exact flag star-and-crescent as a killmark).

5. Iran — Artesh / IRGC fused kit (playable as one faction)
Doctrine: The worst conventional air force of the nine, on purpose. Compensation is industrial: drones that cost nothing, SRBMs that keep coming, dispersed TELs that pack up, and a SAM belt (Bavar / Khordad / Sayyad) that is dangerous until someone spends the air to break it. Playstyle is ‘death by a thousand cuts’ plus one big Fateh/Zolfaghar rain. If Iran wins a fair F-16 dogfight against a Rafale, the designer has failed.
Unique mechanic — SWARM + DISPERSE: Shahed-136 flavour one-way drones are built from a cheap Workshop, not the airfield. They do not return. TELs that fire a ballistic shot can PACK in 8 seconds and look like trucks until they unpack. Killing the Workshop matters more than killing one drone. This is the opposite of Israel’s ‘expensive intercept’ fantasy — it is the reason Iron Dome exists on the other side of the ladder.
Hero — SARTIP-E DOVOM REZA ‘KOUH’ MORADI: Quds-adjacent commando fantasy kept generic (no real org branding). Mountain bonus. Can plant a beacon for one Fateh shot into fog. Voice: Tehran Persian, tight, then filthy.
Superweapon — RAAD-E FATH: mixed Fateh-110 / Zolfaghar / Shahed-136 saturation on a large radius. Buildings burn, infantry die, tanks are hurt not deleted. Interceptable by Iron Dome, Patriot, S-400, Siper, Akash — this is the rock-paper of the roster. Late: Fattah-flavour hypersonic that is HARDER to intercept but tiny radius. Still conventional. No nuclear option, ever.
Colour: desert tan + green-white-red trim used lightly. IRGC and Artesh are fused into one kit so the player is not managing two tech trees.

7. Campaign additions (short)
Each new faction gets a 6-mission mini-campaign, not a 7-mission epic, so writing does not explode. Same Al-Mizan month.
Turkey — ‘KIRMIZI İZ’: drones over a highland border; a MİLGEM must keep a sea lane; finale is a Siper vs cruise-missile night.
Israel — ‘KOVAH BARZEL’: first missions are intercept-the-rain, not tank rushes. Finale is an Adir package on an Al-Mizan bunker, not a city.
India — ‘TRISHUL WARD’: a two-front tutorial without naming the neighbour as a campaign villain in text (the player already knows). BrahMos lane tutorial is mission 3.
Pakistan — ‘GHAZWA ZERO’: first-strike timing school. Hangor mission on a coast. Finale is survive the second volley.
Iran — ‘BARAN’: workshop and TEL dispersal school. If the player builds a fighter deathball they lose. Finale is a rain that must land before Domes/Patriots come up.
Shared finale from v0.1 grows a third mission if any Wave 3 campaign is complete: nine tents, one map, still no speech longer than twenty seconds.

10. Legal / ratings / store page
PEGI 18 / ESRB M remains correct the moment Uncut Hebrew, Turkish, Hindi, Urdu and Persian are in the build.
Store text: ‘Nine regional armed forces. Equipment abstracted from public reporting. All stories fictional.’
Do not ship real news footage in trailers.
Israel and Iran will generate press. The answer is always: they are RTS factions with public kits, the antagonist is Al-Mizan, nukes are off, civilians are not a resource.
India and Pakistan will generate forum wars. Mirror matches and a clean referee (no campaign text that declares a winner of a real war) are the fix.

12. Closing
The original game was an Arabic war game. It still is, at the centre. The five additions make the ladder a region instead of a peninsula. That is a bigger product and a bigger argument. Hold the fence from v0.1: published kits, no nukes, no living politicians, no sectarian slurs, Uncut behind a slider, slice before spectacle.
If the slice is still Egypt’s Kornet versus Algeria’s TOS, the new five will have a game to walk into. If it is not, they will just be five more unfinished tech trees.
